## 🏛️ The Core Architectural Pillars

### 1. The 4-Answer Rule
Every AI finding in Proofline strictly answers:
1. **What is wrong?** (Exact highlighted target phrase)
2. **Why does it matter?** (Regulatory penalty, trademark exposure, or brand dilution rationale)
3. **Which approved rule or document supports it?** (Direct citation to Auditor Statements, FDA letters, or Brand Manuals with page references)
4. **How to fix it?** (Tone-preserving compliant alternative phrases with 1-click replacement)

### 2. Zero-Hallucination Fact-Vault (RAG Engine)
Instead of allowing generative models to invent scale metrics, Proofline indexes official corporate figures (e.g. *$14.2B AUM verified by KPMG Q3 2024 Audit Statement*). When marketing copy asserts *$50B AUM*, Proofline intercepts the claim with critical severity and provides the audited figure.

### 3. Multi-Client Enterprise Architecture
Proofline is a universal governance engine built for any enterprise or agency. Evaluators can switch between 3 pre-loaded industry workspaces in the top header:
* **Northstar Financial (FinTech / Wealth):** Enforces SEC Rule 206(4)-1 non-promissory standards and KPMG $14.2B AUM Fact-Vault.
* **Apex Health Technologies (MedTech / Healthcare):** Enforces FDA 510(k) clearance, prohibits "100% cure rate" and "miracle" claims, and validates clinical cohorts against Lancet Oncology publications.
* **CloudScale Systems (Enterprise Cloud):** Enforces 99.999% SLA availability benchmarks, SOC-2 Type II security lexicon, and prevents cloud absolutism claims.

### 4. Mathematical WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility Engine
Deterministically analyzes visual banners, extracts HEX swatches, and computes exact relative luminance contrast ratios:
$$\text{Contrast Ratio} = \frac{L_1 + 0.05}{L_2 + 0.05}$$
Flags non-compliant assets below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA legal threshold and harmonizes them to approved corporate swatches in 1 click.

### 5. Guideline Friction Radar (Self-Healing Brand Books)
Measures the percentage of times human managers override an AI flag. A high override rate (e.g., 72.5% on promotional superlatives) signals that the guideline itself is ambiguous, enabling managers to deploy automated policy refinements.

## 🚀 Three Major Improvements for Round 3 (Non-Cosmetic Additions)

As committed in the Round 1 proposal, Round 3 will introduce three deep technical capabilities:

### Improvement 1: Multi-Agent LLM Red-Teaming & Adversarial Regulatory Stress-Testing
* **Technical Scope:** Implementation of an autonomous adversarial LLM agent ("Regulatory Prosecutor Agent") that stress-tests marketing copy prior to syndication. It actively probes for safe-harbor loopholes, simulates competitor class-action challenges, and evaluates exposure to SEC/FDA regulatory enforcement inquiries under adversarial prompting.
* **Enterprise Value:** Shifts governance from passive rule checking to proactive legal vulnerability simulation, protecting enterprise balance sheets from multi-million dollar regulatory fines.

### Improvement 2: Vector-Backed Hybrid RAG on Live Enterprise Document Repositories (pgvector / Unstructured 10-K Ingestion)
* **Technical Scope:** Integration of a production document ingestion pipeline using `pgvector` dense semantic embeddings paired with BM25 lexical search. Allows legal teams to drop massive 200+ page quarterly SEC 10-K/10-Q filings, FDA clinical investigator brochures, and financial ledgers directly into Proofline, automatically chunking and vector-indexing claims with cryptographic timestamp citations.
* **Enterprise Value:** Eliminates manual factsheet curation; enterprises ground marketing campaigns directly against their official SEC and regulatory filings with 100% zero-hallucination guarantees.

### Improvement 3: Real-Time Headless CMS & Design Tool Publishing Interceptor (Figma, Canva, HubSpot, WordPress)
* **Technical Scope:** Development of a Chrome Browser Extension and webhook SDK that injects directly into enterprise publishing stacks (HubSpot, WordPress, Hootsuite, and Figma). It intercepts native "Publish" and "Export" buttons in real time, blocking campaign syndication at the browser/API boundary unless a valid Proofline SHA-256 cryptographic compliance pass hash is verified.
* **Enterprise Value:** Prevents unauthorized creator "rogue publishing" by technically locking syndication pipelines to Proofline's Stage-Gate human sign-off authority.
